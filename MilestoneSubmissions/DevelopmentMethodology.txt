Our team will use an Agile development methodology for organizing the four-week development process for Chorify. 
We will divide the project into smaller tasks and delegate them based on each team member’s strengths and skills. 
To stay organized and meet deadlines, we will use a checklist based on the requirements for each weekly submission, allowing us to track completed tasks and identify what still needs to be done and by who.

Throughout development we will communicate regularly to discuss progress, address challenges, and ensure everyone is on track. 
We will prioritize essential features, test functionality as it is developed, and make adjustments as needed. 
This approach will help our team collaborate effectively while staying on schedule, and deliver a functional version of Chorify within the four-week timeline.
