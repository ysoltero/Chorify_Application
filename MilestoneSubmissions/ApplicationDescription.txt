Chorify is an application that gamifies household chores by turning everyday responsibilities into a fun, competitive experience. 
The application allows users to create a household, assign who has admin access, add chores, and assign point values to each task based on its difficulty or effort. 
Everyone in the household can access the shared chores list, view available tasks, and earn points by completing chores.

Chorify keeps track of each member’s points and displays a live leaderboard so users can see who is in the lead. 
By introducing friendly competition and a reward system, Chorify tries to encourage household members to contribute more consistently to shared responsibilities. 
The goal is to make managing chores easier and more engaging for families, roommates, and other shared households.
